Good questions.
Clearer plans.

A few things to consider before arranging your Houston dumpster rental.

What size dumpster do I need?

The right size depends on debris volume, material weight, and placement space. Describe your project and confirm available sizes before renting. Heavy debris can reach a weight limit before a container is full.

What can go in a dumpster?

Accepted materials depend on the rental and disposal requirements. Confirm your exact debris list before loading, especially paint, chemicals, batteries, tires, appliances, or heavy material.

Where should I put the container?

Plan for a stable placement area and enough access for delivery and pickup. Discuss surface protection and overhead clearance. Confirm any property or public-space permissions before placement.

How much will a rental cost?

Pricing depends on the container, materials, rental period, location, and disposal weight. Ask for a complete quote and clarify extra-day charges, weight overages, and restricted-item fees.

How do I get started?

Call (346) 660-2743 with your project address, debris type and preferred dates. We’ll discuss container options, accepted materials, placement and the full rental details.

Can I arrange a specific delivery date?

Confirm availability for your desired date before scheduling project work around it. Container sizes, delivery windows, and pickup arrangements need to be agreed before booking.
